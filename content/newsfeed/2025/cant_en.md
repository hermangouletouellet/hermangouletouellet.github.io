*CANT conference.* From 29th September 2025 to 3rd October 2025, the 5th edition of the CANT school will take place at CIRM in Marseille, France. CANT is an acronym for Combinatorics, Automata, and Number Theory. The first two editions of the school were held in Liège (Belgium) in 2006 and 2009 and brought together 100 and 60 participants respectively. The last two editions both took place at CIRM (Marseille, France) in 2012 and 2016 and welcomed around 100 participants from all over the world.

For more information, go to the web site: <http://www.discmath.ulg.ac.be/CANT2025/>.
