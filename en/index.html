Herman Goulet-Ouellet

Professor of mathematics at Moncton University.

My research centers on algebra and symbolic dynamics. I am interested in what various algebraic methods can reveal about combinatorial and ergodic properties of symbolic systems. For instance, I study the profinite invariants associated with those systems by means of the theory of ideals in semigroups, as well as the distribution of rational languages under ergodic measures using the algebraic theory of automata.

2026/09/19

Leiden Declaration on Artificial Intelligence and Mathematics

The Leiden Declaration on Artificial Intelligence and Mathematics, a statement on the use of AI in mathematics, was released recently. The document is the work of an international group of 16 researchers in mathematics and other relevant fields (computer science, history, philosophy and anthropology) and has been endorsed by the International Mathematical Union. It identifies potential threats posed by AI in mathematical research and formulates concrete recommendations for individual mathematicians, mathematical organizations, and policymakers. For more information and to read the declaration, visit https://leidendeclaration.ai/.

MSCS conference

The Mathematics, Statistics, and Computer Science Conference 2026, organized by Science Atlantic, will take place University of New-Brunswick, Saint-John campus on October 23 and 24, 2026.

The conference will feature three plenary speakers (Danielle Cox, Thierry Duschene et Daniel Lemire), student presentations, as well as the annual Mathematics and Programming competitions (open to undegraduate students). Registration is open until October 5. For more information, see the conference website: https://event.fourwaves.com/mscs2026.
